Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

+

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and follow the instructions sent to your email.

Where can I find my course materials?

+

Course materials are available in the student portal under "My Courses". Select your course to access lectures, assignments, and resources.

How do I contact my professors?

+

Professor contact information is available in the course directory. You can also use the messaging system in the student portal.

What payment methods are accepted?

+

We accept credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and online payment platforms. Visit the payment portal for more options.

How do I apply for scholarships?

+

Scholarship applications are available through the financial aid portal. Check eligibility criteria and submit required documents.
